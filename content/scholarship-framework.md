# From Project to Purpose

PDF: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SqioInJZgKB-8Al-h4vY477c7QfXj8nk/view?usp=sharing

Framework for turning a project into a ~500-word Hong Kong scholarship essay focused on personal motivation and social responsibility.

1. Align with the civic test: who was helped, which Hong Kong problem, why it mattered.
2. Name need, beneficiaries, activities, outcomes, applicant role.
3. Short arc: local hook, initiative name, one human outcome, future commitment.
4. Root motivation in Hong Kong (family, Cantonese/English, density, digital speed).

The PDF's senior digital-inclusion workshop is a writing template, not a claim that it is the only project.
